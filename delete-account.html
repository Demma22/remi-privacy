REMI – Account & Data Deletion

This page explains how users of the REMI mobile application can request deletion of their account and associated personal data.

How to Request Deletion

  1. Open the REMI app
  2. Go to Settings
  3. Under Data Management
  4. Tap Delete Account & Data
  5. Select Contact Support

You will be directed to the REMI support contact page, where you can reach us via email, phone, or WhatsApp to submit your deletion request.

Verification

To protect user privacy and security, we verify account ownership before processing any deletion request.

Data That Will Be Deleted

  • User account and authentication information
  • Academic data including timetables, exams, and GPA records
  • Chat history and AI interaction data

After Deletion

Once the deletion request is completed, all associated data is permanently removed from our systems. To continue using REMI, users must create a new account.
